2. Entry Period

3. How to Enter & Entry Fee

Entrants may enter by completing the online entry form at [ENTRY FORM URL], providing their first name, surname, and (optionally) a valid mobile number, and selecting the number of entries they wish to purchase.

This is a paid competition. Each entry costs [PRICE PER ENTRY, e.g. R20.00], payable in South African Rand. Payment is processed securely by [PAYMENT PROCESSOR NAME, e.g. Yoco/PayFast] via card or Instant EFT. The Promoter does not receive or store card or bank account details — these are handled entirely by the payment processor.

Skill-testing requirement: before payment, every entrant must correctly complete a skill-testing puzzle presented on the entry form. Entry is not possible without solving this puzzle correctly — payment cannot be attempted until it has been completed. This requirement exists because South African law does not permit paid entry into a competition determined purely by chance without a genuine skill element; completing the puzzle is that skill element.

An entry is only valid and complete once payment has been confirmed and a unique ticket ID has been issued. A purchase for which payment fails, is cancelled, or is not confirmed does not constitute a valid entry, and no ticket will be issued.

4. Who May Enter

The competition is open to South African residents, except:

  • Directors, members, partners, employees, agents, or consultants of the Promoter, or of any person or entity that directly or indirectly controls or is controlled by the Promoter;
  • Suppliers of goods or services in connection with this competition; and
  • The immediate family members (spouse, life partner, parent, child, or sibling) of anyone listed above.

Entrants under 18: a person under the age of 18 may be entered into this competition only by a parent or legal guardian, who must be the one to complete payment. The paying adult is the entrant of record for the purposes of these rules and is bound by them; if the child's ticket is drawn as a winner, the prize (or arrangements for its collection) will be communicated to and coordinated through that parent or legal guardian.

By entering, entrants (and, where applicable, the parent or guardian entering on a child's behalf) confirm that none of the exclusions above apply to them.
